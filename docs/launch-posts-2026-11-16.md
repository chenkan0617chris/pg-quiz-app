Got an online assessment invite with the Switch, Digit or Grid Challenge? It isn't only P&G.

These are Aon (cut-e) game-based tests, and P&G is just one of the employers that send them. Most people can solve the questions. What costs marks is the clock: each game is only a few minutes, and accuracy drops if you have only ever practised untimed.

How I'd use the week before the test:
→ Days 1–3: get each question type right, untimed. Check your method, not just the answer.
→ Days 4–5: switch to timed rounds. Aim for steady accuracy, not your fastest attempt.
→ Days 6–7: sit full timed mock tests and review every question you got wrong.

That last step is why I added timed mock tests to CK Quiz: Switch, Digit and Grid Challenge rounds back to back under time pressure, then a question-by-question report.

There's also free verbal reasoning practice (True / False / Cannot Say) for SHL-style tests, plus guides for Aon, SHL, KPMG and EY assessments.
